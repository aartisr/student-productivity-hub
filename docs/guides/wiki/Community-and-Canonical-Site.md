# Community and Canonical Site

The canonical home for Student Productivity Hub is:

## [https://sph.ai-aarti.com](https://sph.ai-aarti.com/)

The GitHub repository and its Pages site exist to make the work inspectable, reusable, and easier to improve. They should always point people back to the canonical product experience.

## Ways To Participate

- Use the app and note where a workflow saves time or creates friction.
- Open an issue with a clear example, expected behavior, and context.
- Improve a guide when you discover a better explanation.
- Contribute a focused change with test evidence.
- Share feedback from classrooms, study groups, and accessibility contexts.

## Community Standard

Thoughtful disagreement and precise feedback are welcome. Please keep participation respectful and constructive by following the [Code of Conduct](../../community/code-of-conduct.md).

For contributor expectations, see the [Contributing Guide](../../community/contributing.md).
