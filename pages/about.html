About the project

A study tool should respect attention.

Student Productivity Hub is an open, mobile-first workspace for the small decisions that shape a learning day: what needs attention, what comes next, when to focus, and how to practice with feedback.

What we are trying to improve

Most students do not need another noisy dashboard. They need a dependable place to hold commitments, begin a focused session, practice understanding, and recover when a plan changes.

The product connects those moments through planning tools, focused sessions, portable quizzes, review signals, and local-first backups. It is deliberately built as a project that can be examined, challenged, and improved.

Where it began

With gratitude to all teachers of PCSS II for making this possible, and a special shout out to Mr. Shaol, Computer Science Teacher, for starting this as a regular class project. The first version did not need to be perfect; it needed a place to begin.
